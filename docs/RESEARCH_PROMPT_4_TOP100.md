## How the families were formed and ranked

A family is a post's title with its multiplicity and any parenthetical removed, and a
trailing `, VISN N …` or `, Region N …` removed. A family ranks by the number of unpriced
nodes it holds, ties broken by the number of organisations they sit in. Grouping posts by
title is not a claim that they are paid alike; the prompt asks for one line per employer
group wherever the pay system differs.

**Nothing an answer returns is published until a matcher reads it out of a committed
document.** An answer names a document to fetch; it is never itself the citation.
