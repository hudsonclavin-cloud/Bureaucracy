A lead is an unpriced post the matching rules have not tied to a printed row (it carries no listing) whose title the export prints under the post's OWN agency, in an organisation no node has been matched to or one in the post's own chain; or, where the agency is one this graph has no node for, in an organisation named for the post's own parent. It is a question for a person, never a claim: the rules refuse to guess which of several organisations' office this is. Say "that one" and a reviewed row follows (the lead stays open, marked awaiting a row, until it exists); say "none of them" and the decision is recorded in `data/curation/pay_lead_decisions.json` with the reason and closes the lead.
